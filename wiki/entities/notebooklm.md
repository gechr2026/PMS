# NotebookLM

Google's document-Q&A product. Cited in the source as a representative of the **pure-RAG** model — uploads + query-time retrieval, with no persistent compiled synthesis between sources.[^src1]

## Why it's named here

The source uses NotebookLM (alongside ChatGPT file uploads) as the counter-example to the [[llm-wiki-pattern]]: "the LLM is rediscovering knowledge from scratch on every question. There's no accumulation."[^src1] See [[rag-vs-persistent-wiki]] for the full contrast.
