# Memex

A hypothetical personal knowledge store proposed by [[vannevar-bush]] in 1945. Conceived as a private, curated repository with **associative trails** linking documents — a vision much closer to a personal wiki than to what the open web became.[^src1]

## Significance in this wiki

The [[llm-wiki-pattern]] is positioned as the realization of Bush's vision, with the missing piece — sustained maintenance — supplied by an LLM rather than a human.[^src1]

## Key claims

- Bush's vision emphasized **private, actively curated** knowledge, not public broadcast.[^src1]
- The **connections between documents** were as valuable as the documents themselves.[^src1]
- Bush could not solve **who does the maintenance** at scale.[^src1]
