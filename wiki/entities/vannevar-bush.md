# Vannevar Bush

American engineer and science administrator (1890–1974), best known in this wiki as the originator of the [[memex]] concept in his 1945 essay "As We May Think."

## Why he matters here

The [[llm-wiki-pattern]] is described as a "spiritual descendant" of Bush's [[memex]] — a vision of a private, actively curated knowledge store with associative trails between documents.[^src1] The article frames it bluntly: "The part he couldn't solve was who does the maintenance. The LLM handles that."[^src1]
